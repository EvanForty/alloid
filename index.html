Good discovery leaves room for doubt.

Each candidate is scored by simple, published rules of thumb. Your report separates what those rules estimate from what only a furnace can tell you.

CandidateDiscarded
What this agent cannot know
  • Whether brittle intermetallic compounds form — that needs CALPHAD or DFT calculations.
  • Real strength, toughness or creep life, which depend on processing and microstructure.
  • Oxidation and corrosion behaviour beyond the presence of oxide-forming elements.
  • Cost, supply risk and whether the alloy can be cast or machined at all.
